[h1]The Best Lockpicking [B42.20][/h1]

A complete lockpicking overhaul for Project Zomboid Build 42.20. Break into locked buildings and vehicles using bobby pins or a crowbar — with a dedicated Lockpicking skill that grows as you practice.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Bobby Pin Minigame[/h2]

Pick locks the realistic way using a pin tumbler simulation. Set each pin to the shear line by finding the right tension range. Listen for the click. Too much tension breaks your pick.

[b]New Mode (default)[/b] — Full pin-by-pin picking. The binding order changes per lock. Mushroom security pins create false sets. Trembling hands at low skill make tension control harder.

[b]Old Mode[/b] — A simpler chance-based system. Good for servers that want faster gameplay.

Controls: [b]A / D[/b] to adjust tension — [b]Mouse[/b] to move the pick

[h2]Crowbar Unlocking[/h2]

Force open locked doors and vehicle doors. Requires Strength ≥ 4 and enough endurance. Damages the door permanently. The animation and result replicate for all players in multiplayer.

Controls: [b]Space[/b] to apply force

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Lockpicking Skill[/h2]

A dedicated [b]Lockpicking[/b] skill appears in the [b]Survivalist[/b] skill tree. XP is earned from every pick attempt — successful or failed. Higher skill means:

[list]
[*]Lower chance of breaking your pick
[*]Lower chance of breaking the lock
[*]Harder locks become accessible
[*]Unlocked at level 0, mastered at level 10
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Skill Books[/h2]

Five Lockpicking skill books are distributed in the world and let you earn XP faster:

[list]
[*]Lockpicking for Beginners

[*]Lockpicking Vol. 3
[*]Advanced Lockpicking Vol. 4
[*]Advanced Lockpicking Vol. 5
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Alarm Checking[/h2]

Before committing to a pick, check if the building or vehicle has an alarm. Avoids triggering a horde by accident.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Crafting[/h2]

Handmade bobby pins can be crafted once you know the recipe:

[list]
[*][b]1 bobby pin[/b] — Screwdriver (keep) + 1 Paperclip
[*][b]10 bobby pins[/b] — Screwdriver (keep) + Wire (keep - until we use it all)
[/list]

[h2]Professions & Traits[/h2]

[table]
[tr]
[th]Who[/th][th]Cost[/th][th]Bonus[/th]
[/tr]
[tr]
[td]Burglar profession[/td][td]costs 6 pts[/td][td]+2× XP for Nimble, Sneak, Lightfoot [b]and[/b] Lockpicking · full lockpicking & alarm-check recipes · bobby pin crafting · many extra recipes (armor, shivs, key copy...) · [b]hotwire access[/b][/td]
[/tr]
[tr]
[td]Locksmith trait[/td][td]costs 3 pts[/td][td]+2× Lockpicking XP only · lockpicking & alarm-check recipes · bobby pin crafting[/td]
[/tr]
[/table]

[b]Burglar and Locksmith are mutually exclusive.[/b] Pick Burglar for a full stealth/criminal build — it also unlocks the [b]hotwire minigame[/b]. Pick Locksmith if you are playing another profession but still want lockpicking capability — it costs half as many points and leaves the rest of your build open. Characters who skip both can still level Lockpicking through practice; they just start without the recipes. Hotwire is available to the Burglar profession [b]or[/b] to any character with [b]Electrical 1 + Mechanics 2[/b] (same as vanilla).
